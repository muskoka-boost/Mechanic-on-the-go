### Replace this as soon as there is a real photo

This is a placeholder standing in for the owner's own work. A generic stock car
is the weakest possible hero for a local trade business: the whole argument the
site makes is "this is a real person who turns up in your driveway," and stock
photography actively works against that. The moment a full-resolution photo of
the owner's own work exists, it should replace this.

`public/images/hero-real.jpg` holds the owner's Ram 1500 driveway shot, ready to
swap back in — see `scripts/process-photos.mjs`.
